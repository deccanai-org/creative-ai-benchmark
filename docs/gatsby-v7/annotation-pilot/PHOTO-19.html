Cascadia Founders Summit - Executive portrait set

Client brief

Portrait cards for Cascadia Founders Summit Our speakers and hosts have sent photographs taken in very different settings. We want them to look like part of one event without making the people look alike. This commission is for portrait cards and a summit announcement; the older square and stage-screen portrait placements in summit_brand_notes.txt are background references, not additional exports. Please create eighteen 1080 x 1350 px portrait cards: fourteen speaker cards and four host cards, as named in the requested deliverables. Match each person to their own portrait and record in speakers.csv. Set their name as the largest text, then include the role line, affiliation and session from that record. Keep the role readable on a phone and retain the host roles on the four host cards. Use cfs_mark_transparent.svg for the CFS mark. We also need one 1920 x 1080 px announcement for the summit itself, not an individual speaker confirmation. "Cascadia Founders Summit" should be the headline and largest text. Include the approved event date and a short, readable invitation to register. We have not supplied a registration address or deadline; please leave those out. Do not introduce a price beyond the 600 to 1,200 dollar pass range in the brand identity. Use the supplied copy and records alongside the current brand identity. Follow programme_copy.txt where it corrects programme wording or permissions in the records. Deliver the nineteen named files. Supplied identity artwork cfs_mark_transparent.svg supplies reusable CFS artwork. The older summit_badge_2025_PRINTER.pdf remains a layout reference only; no mark extraction is required. What stays fixed Match each person to their own portrait and approved programme record. Retain fourteen speaker and four host roles. On portrait cards the person's name is largest; on the announcement the summit name is largest. Your design decisions Choose crops, restrained retouching and a consistent background treatment that preserves identity. Arrange programme information for phone reading; choose announcement composition without inventing registration details. Delivery and fonts Use the placement and export instructions listed with each requested file below. The downloadable OFL fonts in Source assets are the current typefaces for this commission; the typography card gives their roles and weights.

Brand identity

Cascadia Founders Summit

Cascadia Founders Summit is a two-day gathering for founders and the people who back them. Passes run six hundred to twelve hundred dollars and the room is roughly four hundred people who read the calibre of the event off its details. The summit sells seriousness and access rather than spectacle, and everything it puts in front of that audience is meant to look considered.

Audience

Founders, operators and institutional investors aged 28 to 55 who expense the ticket and judge the event by how professional it feels.

Positioning

Delegate passes 600 to 1,200 dollars, a credible professional summit priced above a free meetup and below a flagship conference. Restraint reads as calibre; a glossy or busy treatment would misprice it.

Colour rules

Distribution
Grounds and panels are Warm Paper or Cascadia Slate and nothing else; the ground behind each sitter is the portrait's house ground (see photography). On Warm Paper all type is Near Black, apart from an affiliation line in Signal Copper, as on summit_badge_2025_PRINTER.pdf. On Cascadia Slate all type is Warm Paper or Fog Grey, and the CFS mark is Fog Grey on Cascadia Slate, as on the badge. Signal Copper (the affiliation line, thin rules) and Evergreen (thin rules and dividers only) are accents, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground, a panel, a speaker name, a role line or a headline.
Opacity
Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: a text panel sits over or beside the portrait as a solid block. Supplied raster identity artwork retains its edge anti-aliasing. Place it at 100% layer opacity; colour rules apply to any intentional recolouring.
Not allowed
No gloss, glow, drop shadow, outline, gradient, metallic or busy texture on any type, panel, rule, button or icon, since restraint reads as calibre and a glossy or busy treatment would misprice the room; no coloured or busy ground behind any sitter; and no colour outside the six palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules, buttons or icons, apart from the ground behind a sitter, which may be any soft, even tone in the Fog Grey #D9DDDE to Warm Paper #F2EEE7 range (summit_brand_notes.txt).
Photography
Portraits keep natural skin and their subjects' own colouring and are never recoloured toward the palette. The one palette-range tone in a portrait is its ground: a soft, even tone within the Fog Grey #D9DDDE to Warm Paper #F2EEE7 range, the same behind every sitter (summit_brand_notes.txt). Mixed-light casts, such as the green-tinged fluorescent light of speaker_portrait_04.jpg and staff_portrait_03.jpg, the warm lamplight of speaker_portrait_07.jpg and the cool open shade of speaker_portrait_12.jpg, are brought to natural skin, with no glossy warm filter and no flat, hard direct-flash look, and are not pushed toward Signal Copper or Evergreen.
