# Aldervale Mutual

## Brand name

Aldervale Mutual

## Tagline

Owned by the people we cover.

## Sector

A policyholder owned mutual life insurer selling whole life and final expense through appointed producers.

## Founded place size

A mutual insurer serving households in central Vermont through appointed producers.

## About

Aldervale Mutual is a policyholder-owned life insurer serving households in central Vermont. Its local producers need clear, dependable materials that explain cover and contact details without a hard sell. Accurate disclosure wording is central to the work.

## Personality

- Sober
- Trustworthy
- Plain spoken
- Institutional
- Steady
- Respectful

## Audience

Households aged 35 to 60 with modest savings, buying because they do not want to leave a burden. They trust plainness and distrust anything that reads like a sales pitch.

## Price positioning

A regulated mutual with premiums from 45 to 180 dollars a month. Materials should feel accessible and dependable, neither like private-wealth advertising nor discount direct-response mail.

## Voice

Sober, plain and reassuring, written the way a trusted advisor speaks across a table. No exclamation, no urgency. Example line the brand would write: A whole life policy is a promise that holds its value, and the premium you start with is the premium you keep.

## Brand assets status

No separate logo file is required by this card commission. Use the Aldervale Mutual name as text in the supplied identity. Rebuilding the card layout from the press PDF is explicitly included; creating a new logo is not.

## Values

Nothing may read as an advertisement, and everything must survive the kitchen table. Compliance and honesty come before flourish, and the material should feel as dependable as the carrier behind it.

## Palette rules

- Distribution: Document Paper is the card ground and the only ground or panel colour, as on countercard_2026_print.pdf. All type is Ink Navy or Slate: the producer name and the Disclosure Text in Ink Navy and the license line and office in Slate, as on countercard_2026_print.pdf; any premium in either. Heritage Green is only the two 1 pt rules that bracket the card. Mist Grey is only for thin dividers or a keyline. Heritage Green and Mist Grey together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area. Palette hex values define sRGB source colours. In the print PDF, convert them with PSOcoated_v3.icc; evaluate the colour-managed proof, not literal RGB hex values inside the CMYK file.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons. No type in Heritage Green, Mist Grey or Document Paper, no premium picked out in any colour, bold or italic, and no burst or starburst callout in any colour, as compliance_note.txt rules out.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own natural colour and skin tones and are never tinted, duotoned or recoloured toward the palette. Neutralising a phone's warm or cool cast is allowed; pushing a suit, a face or a wall toward Ink Navy, Slate, Heritage Green or Mist Grey is not.

## Creative scope

- Fixed: Use the approved producer, Office and full Disclosure Text for each record. Do not add a district line. The old press PDF establishes field coverage, while the current identity sets type and colour. Never substitute someone else's headshot for a missing portrait.
- Designer choices: Rebuild the card grid, portrait crop and text hierarchy for legibility. Keep required facts and disclosure verbatim; flag missing source portraits to the client before release rather than inventing a replacement.
