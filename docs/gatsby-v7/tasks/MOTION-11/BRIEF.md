# SB3-091-MOT | STATIC FOX - Thumbnail and animated channel package

Channel pack time. Six thumbnails, the title animation, and a feed teaser cut from the captures.

The six captures are five seconds each and they are silent, which is fine, because nobody hears the feed anyway. Everything the viewer gets is the text you set on screen. Nobody talks in any of this, so do not put a voice or a caption of a voice on it. A cleared instrumental bed is in the pack as staticfox_bed.wav, and it is the music bed on both teasers.

What we need back

Channel thumbnails: six files, one per upload title, each title exactly as written in the titles file and each built on a moment from the capture that upload title belongs to. Two of the titles are enormous and four are tiny and they still have to look like one channel. Title and mascot fight nothing, including each other, and the title still reads at 320 px wide in a feed.

Animated channel title and end card: one file, wide, six to eight seconds, silent, authored from the identity rather than cut from gameplay. Give the reveal some intent and land on a final frame we can actually use as an end card, with the members line on it complete and all three lines of the end card copy.

Channel feed teaser: one file, wide, twelve to fifteen seconds, music bed. Cut it from at least four of the six captures, with the tagline and the upload line from the end card copy set on screen. No invented footage, and no looping one capture to fill the length.

Channel feed teaser upright cutdown: one file, upright, six to eight seconds, music bed, from the same cut.

Use staticfox_scrap_transparent.png as the supplied mascot artwork, without redesigning or stretching it. Titles, tagline, members line and end card copy all come off the titles file exactly as written.

Use staticfox_bed.wav for both teasers, with no speech or capture audio. The upright teaser uses at least three of the captures used in the wide teaser. Title 1 belongs to capture 01, title 2 to capture 02, and so on through title 6 and capture 06. For capture 04, keep the webcam window whole, cover it fully, or crop it out; do not leave a partly cut face. Use the exact members line and all three end-card lines from staticfox_titles.txt.

Supplied identity artwork
staticfox_scrap_transparent.png is the supplied reusable mascot cutout. staticfox_scrap_render.png and staticfox_patch.jpg are historical references only; mascot extraction and patch reconstruction are not additional deliverables. Preserve the cutout's orange fur, purple headset and silhouette.

What stays fixed
Use the exact titles and end-card copy in staticfox_titles.txt. Match thumbnail titles 1-6 to captures 01-06. Use the supplied Scrap cutout without redesign or stretching. Capture footage keeps its own game colours.

Your design decisions
Choose compelling capture moments, thumbnail composition, reveal timing, motion paths, transitions and the music edit. Build the title animation from identity elements; do not substitute a gameplay montage for it.

Delivery and fonts
Use the placement and export instructions listed with each requested file below. The downloadable OFL fonts in Source assets are the current typefaces for this commission; the typography card gives their roles and weights.
