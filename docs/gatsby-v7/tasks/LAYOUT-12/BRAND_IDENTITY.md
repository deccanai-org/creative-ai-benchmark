# Umbra Loft

## Brand name

Umbra Loft

## Tagline

Downstairs, after dark.

## Sector

An independent two hundred and fifty capacity basement live music room programming a nine night autumn jazz and after dark series.

## Founded place size

Founded 2021, a basement room under a former textile mill in Providence, Rhode Island, a small independent team.

## About

Umbra Loft is a two hundred and fifty capacity basement room under an old mill in Providence, programming jazz and after dark nights for a crowd that finds us through friends and phones at eleven at night. This autumn we are running nine nights as one series rather than nine separate shows. It has to look like one thing, and it has to look like the room it actually is: dark, close and made by hand.

## Personality

- dark
- high contrast
- raw
- atmospheric
- handmade
- underground

## Audience

A scene literate, discovery driven crowd aged twenty one to thirty five, reached almost entirely on a phone late at night, who read polish as inauthentic and reward work that plainly comes from the room.

## Price positioning

Tickets 18 to 32 dollars, capacity 250. Independent basement scale: deliberately made and high contrast, credible at a headline ticket without ever reading as corporate or sponsored programming.

## Typography

Use Sora ExtraBold caps for series titles, act names and date lines. The A2 series title is 180 pt / 148 pt with -10 tracking; the release-post title is 96 px. On A2 posters, Sable Quartet, Meridian Brass and Night Ferry are each larger than the remaining act names; choose their larger size to fit the direction. The remaining act names are 48 pt. All release-post act names are 76 px. Act names use -10 tracking and one or two lines; KOA stays on one line. Act names are Bone White on Pitch. Date lines are Sodium, immediately above their act, at 18 pt / 22 pt on posters and 30 px / 36 px on posts, with +20 tracking. Archivo Narrow Regular sets body, supporting billing and tables: 14 pt / 19 pt on posters, 28 px / 38 px on posts and 18 pt / 25 pt in the deck, with 0 tracking and at most 65 characters per line. Poster licence and accessibility lines use Archivo Narrow Regular at 9 pt / 13 pt. Release-post fine print is at least 22 px / 30 px. Fine print uses +30 tracking. Archivo Narrow SemiBold is reserved for door-time information; other fine print is Regular.

## Voice

Terse, atmospheric, unvarnished. It names the act, the night and the door and gets out of the way. Example line the brand would write: "Doors 8. Downstairs. Two sets, one room, cash on the night."

Rules:
- Act names use one or two lines as needed; KOA stays on one line. Do not apply the 180 pt series-title size to the full billing list.

## Palette rules

- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Pitch or Ash and nothing else; Pitch is the main ground and always the ground under an act block. Every act name is Bone White on Pitch with its date line directly above it in Sodium, as the type rules require. Every other line of type, titles, headings, support, door, set, body, billing, table and fine-print lines included, is Bone White; the advance price may be Sodium, as on umbra_seasoncard_autumn.pdf. Pitch and Ash are never type. Cold Signal and Blood are accents for hairline rules, dividers and chart marks only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never type, a ground or a panel. Any palette colour may be one of the two colours of a photograph's treatment. Supplied production act wordmarks are recoloured to Bone White on Pitch; their letter shapes remain unchanged.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, for type, grounds, panels, rules, chart marks and recovered act wordmarks: no tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, laid over a photograph. The translucent type and rule of last season's umbra_seasoncard_autumn.pdf (printed at 86%, 78%, 70% and 55% opacity) are not carried over. The one place two palette colours mix is inside a photograph, in its two-colour treatment. Supplied raster identity artwork retains its edge anti-aliasing. Place it at 100% layer opacity; colour rules apply to any intentional recolouring.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules, chart marks, icons or recovered act wordmarks; a recovered act wordmark takes one solid palette colour. No sponsor logo or corporate partner mark, since the type rules forbid anything that reads as corporate or sponsored programming.
- Photography: Every photograph carries the series two-colour and grain treatment: rendered in two palette colours with visible grain, never in full colour, and the nine night release posts share one pair so six different rigs read as one series. Photographs are used as shot, with nothing generated, extended, added or painted in.

## Creative scope

- Fixed: Keep the approved billing order, all nine dates, support acts, times and CSV prices. Use the current palette and supplied fonts. Sable Quartet and Meridian Brass have supplied production wordmarks; Night Ferry and the remaining acts are type-only.
- Designer choices: Develop three genuinely different type-led, image-led and graphic-led poster directions. Choose the two-colour photographic pair and grain treatment within the palette, then choose one direction to adapt consistently to the nine release posts. Explain that choice in the deck. Choose the three poster headliner sizes above 48 pt; the remaining poster act names stay at 48 pt.
