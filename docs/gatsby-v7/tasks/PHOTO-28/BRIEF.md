# SB3-028-PHO | Verda Reformer Studio - Fitness campaign image system

Verda's second-studio launch

We are opening a second reformer studio on Calloway Row. The launch should feel calm, welcoming and grounded in the room we actually have. The two room photographs are dusky; please improve them rather than replacing them with an invented studio.

We need three pieces. The 1440 x 2400 px launch-page composition should show our room using at least one of the two supplied photographs, identify the correct location, and present the classes we teach with their prices from roster_2027.csv and a clear booking action. Read the roster alongside owner_note.txt and follow the owner's corrections and permissions.

For the 1080 x 1350 px class announcement, pair the pictured instructor with their own class, price and validity from the roster. Give the instructor's full name as listed and place the cutout on solid Warm Off White or Sage.

The 1080 x 1920 px booking story should make the studio identity readable and keep the booking action inside the safe area specified below.

Use verda_wordmark_transparent.png on all three pieces, keeping its drawn letterforms rather than retyping them. The supplied drawing takes precedence over the written font description for the wordmark. Keep it on one line, at most 34 px tall measured on the visible letterforms; on the launch page, place it above the headline as a separate lockup. Use the attached identity for the rest of the design and deliver the three named PNGs.

Supplied identity artwork
verda_wordmark_transparent.png replaces the low-resolution white-backed verda_logo_web.png for this commission. Keep the supplied letterforms, scale proportionally and use on a light solid ground. The old web export is reference only. No wordmark reconstruction is required.

What stays fixed
Use the actual supplied studio photographs and correctly matched instructor information. owner_note.txt governs corrections to roster_2027.csv. Preserve the supplied Verda letterforms and the story's safe area.

Your design decisions
Choose the room crop and restrained lighting correction, the page's information hierarchy and the booking-action placement. Use the specified solid ground for the instructor cutout; do not invent a different studio.

Delivery and fonts
Use the placement and export instructions listed with each requested file below. The downloadable OFL fonts in Source assets are the current typefaces for this commission; the typography card gives their roles and weights.
