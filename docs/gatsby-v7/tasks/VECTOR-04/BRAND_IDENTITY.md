# Meridian and Ash

## Brand name

Meridian and Ash

## Tagline

Roasted in the machine shop

## Sector

Specialty coffee roaster and cafe selling stickers and merchandise at the till, at farmers markets and to wholesale accounts.

## Founded place size

Founded 2016, Portland, Oregon, a converted machine shop with one counter, two market stalls and roughly forty wholesale accounts.

## About

Meridian and Ash roast single origin coffee in a converted machine shop and sell it across their own counter, at two farmers markets and to about forty wholesale accounts. The merchandise table is half the fun: stickers people put on laptops and water bottles because they actually like the mark. The whole point is a range that feels made by hand, not bought by the case.

## Personality

- Playful
- Generous
- Hand made
- Warm
- Unfussy
- Local

## Audience

Cafe regulars twenty to thirty five who buy a sticker at the till without deciding first, and wholesale buyers who hand them out with orders.

## Price positioning

Impulse retail, three dollars a single and nine dollars a four pack. Priced to be handed out by the fistful, so the range has to read as abundant rather than precious.

## Typography

Meridian and Ash runs on DM Serif Display Regular for display and Libre Franklin for everything else. Step 01, display, is DM Serif Display Regular capitals at 18 pt on 20 pt leading tracked 0.030 em on the arc, the 55 mm sticker setting at near 150 px cap height in a 1600 px proof, with MERIDIAN and ASH arched on a fixed 40 degree curve and the ampersand between them at 1.6 times their cap height, hanging below the arc into the artwork. Step 02, subheading, is DM Serif Display Regular capitals at 11 pt on 13 pt leading tracked 0.020 em for counter card and four pack headers, set flat and off the arc. Step 03, body, is Libre Franklin Regular 400 at 7 pt on 10.5 pt leading, tracking zero, set to a maximum of 65 characters, carrying the supporting line under the wordmark and all sticker copy. Step 04, fine print, is Libre Franklin Bold 700 capitals at 6 pt on 8.5 pt leading tracked 0.040 em for gang sheet labels and die line notes. The ampersand is never set at cap height.

## Voice

Plain, warm, a little wry, never precious. Example line: Pull one off the sheet, stick it on your laptop, come back for the other three.

## Brand assets status

This commission explicitly includes paid reconstruction of the window mark and three bag illustrations from the supplied photographs. Those photographs are tracing references, not ready-to-place logos. They are intentionally retained: supplying finished vectors would remove the central work requested. No redesign of the four originals is authorised; the two companion stickers are the original-design portion.

## Values

Consistency people can trust, so the artwork stops being redrawn slightly differently every batch, and a hand made feel that reads as a real roastery rather than a franchise.

## Signature type move

A single oversized ampersand set at 1.6 times the cap height of MERIDIAN and ASH, hanging below the arc of the wordmark into the artwork, with the two words arched on a fixed 40 degree curve. At arm's length the ampersand is seen first.

Rules:
- Never set the ampersand at cap height. It is always 1.6 times the cap height of MERIDIAN and ASH.
- The ampersand always hangs below the arc, into the artwork, never sitting on the baseline.
- MERIDIAN and ASH always arch on a fixed 40 degree curve, at every size.
- DM Serif Display Regular is display only; Libre Franklin Regular 400 sets sticker copy and Libre Franklin Bold 700 the gang sheet capitals.
- Keep the range reading abundant rather than precious: it is priced to be handed out by the fistful.
- Hand made, not franchise: the artwork is drawn once and reused, never redrawn slightly differently each batch.


## Palette rules

- Distribution: Each recovered design keeps the ink it was painted or printed in: Roast Brown for the wordmark in meridian_window_mark.jpg and the moth on meridian_bag_moth.jpg, Ash Green for the roundel in meridian_window_mark.jpg and the pour-over on meridian_bag_pourover.jpg, Warm Cream for the leaf and hairline ring in meridian_window_mark.jpg, Brick Red for the ridge line on meridian_bag_ridgeline.jpg. Each companion takes its main ink from Roast Brown, Ash Green and Brick Red. Kraft and Warm Cream are secondary: backing shapes, detail reversed out of an ink, or fine lines such as a hairline ring, never the main ink. Ink Black is fine detail only, no more than 5% of a sticker's inked area. Type on a light ground is Roast Brown, or Ash Green for a secondary line; type on Roast Brown or Ash Green is Warm Cream, as on meridian_shelfcard_2025.pdf. Sticker backgrounds stay transparent. Palette hex values define sRGB source colours. In the print PDF, convert them with PSOcoated_v3.icc; evaluate the colour-managed proof, not literal RGB hex values inside the CMYK file.
- Opacity: Every palette colour on the stickers, proofs and gang sheet is used solid, at 100% opacity; no tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode. A proof is transparent only outside the artwork and in its open, uninked negative space; every inked area is fully opaque, and partial transparency occurs only as anti-aliasing along edges, with no white or coloured rectangle behind it.
- Forbidden: No colour outside the six palette hexes for any artwork, type, backing shape, label or mark. The cut paths on the CutContour layer are production lines, not artwork, and are exempt. No colour is sampled from a photograph.
- Photography: No photograph appears in any deliverable. meridian_window_mark.jpg, meridian_bag_moth.jpg, meridian_bag_pourover.jpg and meridian_bag_ridgeline.jpg are tracing references only: each ink is matched to its palette hex rather than sampled.

## Creative scope

- Fixed: Recover the four supplied painted/printed designs faithfully as vector paths. Preserve photographed lettering rather than retyping it. Follow the 55 mm artwork scale, 2.5 mm contour offset, 3 mm minimum corner radius and printer sheet spacing.
- Designer choices: Draw two original companion illustrations with subject matter and visual rhythm that fit the coffee collection. Choose imposition within the printer's clearances. Newly set lettering follows the supplied type system; photographed lettering retains its original shape.
