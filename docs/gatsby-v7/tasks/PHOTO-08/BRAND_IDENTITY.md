# Meridian Motors

## Brand name

Meridian Motors

## Tagline

Certified, and nothing to hide

## Sector

Certified pre-owned car dealership taking roughly six vehicles a week onto the lot.

## Founded place size

An independent certified pre-owned dealership in Portland, Maine, photographing every car in house across three lot bays.

## About

Meridian Motors sells certified pre-owned vehicles between 22,000 and 38,000 dollars to families replacing an ageing car. The listings are the whole business, and the visual argument is simple: this is an orderly operation with nothing to hide. Every car should look as if it were photographed in one clean, evenly lit showroom, with honest paint and legible plain information.

## Personality

- organised
- honest
- plain
- trustworthy
- orderly

## Audience

Buyers aged 30 to 55 replacing a ten year old family car, deciding on trust before they decide on the vehicle.

## Price positioning

22,000 to 38,000 dollars certified pre-owned. The treatment is an even, neutral, well lit showroom white with the full vehicle in frame and honest colour. Never a dark dramatic studio look, which reads as evasive at this price, and never hot saturated pictures with starbursts and a price slash, which read as a buy here pay here lot.

## Palette rules

- Distribution: Showroom White #F6F7F8 is the ground behind all type on every card and on the campaign. Meridian Navy #1F2A3A is the only other ground, for a band, panel or seal carrying reversed type, and never sits behind a vehicle. Prices, mileage and listing detail always sit on Showroom White, never on a Meridian Navy band. On Showroom White, prices, headings, labels and the dealership name are Meridian Navy, mileage and listing detail are Graphite, and fine print is Graphite or, as the one small muted line, Steel Grey. On Meridian Navy, type is Showroom White, except small seal lettering, which may be Steel Grey as on the certification seal of meridian_window_card_autumn.pdf. Compass Teal and Steel Grey are for rules, keylines, seal detail (that seal's Compass Teal compass needles and Steel Grey lettering) and one small muted line only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: a band, panel or seal sits beside or over the photograph as a solid block.
- Forbidden: No starburst, no price slash and no hot saturated sale colour on any type, panel, rule, button or icon, which read as a buy here pay here lot; no dark dramatic studio ground behind a vehicle; and no colour outside the 5 palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons; the supplied roundel artwork (meridian_roundel_transparent.svg, meridian_roundel_reversed.svg, meridian_roundel_transparent.png and meridian_roundel_reversed.png) keeps its own colours.
- Photography: Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette. The three bays' casts (warm apron, yellow-orange wash bay, dim cool-green back row) are corrected to one even, neutral showroom white, but each car keeps its true paint: the Ardmoor Ridge stays deep red and does not swing orange, the black Brookmere Sable keeps its shadow-side detail, and the pale rooflines of the white Norsted Alta and the silver Calder Kestrel are not clipped into the background.

## Creative scope

- Fixed: Keep each vehicle's paint, body geometry, visible condition and stock-record facts. Use the supplied transparent compass artwork; do not redesign it. Marketplace primary-image restrictions do not apply to these branded sales placements.
- Designer choices: Choose the matching vehicle view, crop, consistent showroom finishing and information layout. On the campaign, choose the arrangement of the six cars while keeping each identifiable.
