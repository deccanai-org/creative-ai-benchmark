# Cascadia Founders Summit

## Brand name

Cascadia Founders Summit

## Tagline

Founders and the people who fund them

## Sector

A paid two-day founders summit in Portland, Oregon, for founders, operators and institutional investors.

## Founded place size

Founded 2024, Portland, Oregon, now in its second edition, run by a core team of four staff for a room of about four hundred.

## About

Cascadia Founders Summit is a two-day gathering for founders and the people who back them. Passes run six hundred to twelve hundred dollars and the room is roughly four hundred people who read the calibre of the event off its details. The summit sells seriousness and access rather than spectacle, and everything it puts in front of that audience is meant to look considered.

## Personality

- understated
- credible
- professional
- quiet
- serious
- considered

## Audience

Founders, operators and institutional investors aged 28 to 55 who expense the ticket and judge the event by how professional it feels.

## Price positioning

Delegate passes 600 to 1,200 dollars, a credible professional summit priced above a free meetup and below a flagship conference. Restraint reads as calibre; a glossy or busy treatment would misprice it.

## Palette rules

- Distribution: Grounds and panels are Warm Paper or Cascadia Slate and nothing else; the ground behind each sitter is the portrait's house ground (see photography). On Warm Paper all type is Near Black, apart from an affiliation line in Signal Copper, as on summit_badge_2025_PRINTER.pdf. On Cascadia Slate all type is Warm Paper or Fog Grey, and the CFS mark is Fog Grey on Cascadia Slate, as on the badge. Signal Copper (the affiliation line, thin rules) and Evergreen (thin rules and dividers only) are accents, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground, a panel, a speaker name, a role line or a headline.
- Opacity: Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: a text panel sits over or beside the portrait as a solid block. Supplied raster identity artwork retains its edge anti-aliasing. Place it at 100% layer opacity; colour rules apply to any intentional recolouring.
- Forbidden: No gloss, glow, drop shadow, outline, gradient, metallic or busy texture on any type, panel, rule, button or icon, since restraint reads as calibre and a glossy or busy treatment would misprice the room; no coloured or busy ground behind any sitter; and no colour outside the six palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules, buttons or icons, apart from the ground behind a sitter, which may be any soft, even tone in the Fog Grey #D9DDDE to Warm Paper #F2EEE7 range (summit_brand_notes.txt).
- Photography: Portraits keep natural skin and their subjects' own colouring and are never recoloured toward the palette. The one palette-range tone in a portrait is its ground: a soft, even tone within the Fog Grey #D9DDDE to Warm Paper #F2EEE7 range, the same behind every sitter (summit_brand_notes.txt). Mixed-light casts, such as the green-tinged fluorescent light of speaker_portrait_04.jpg and staff_portrait_03.jpg, the warm lamplight of speaker_portrait_07.jpg and the cool open shade of speaker_portrait_12.jpg, are brought to natural skin, with no glossy warm filter and no flat, hard direct-flash look, and are not pushed toward Signal Copper or Evergreen. The portrait-background neutral range is any single RGB blend from Fog Grey (217,221,222) to Warm Paper (242,238,231); sampled flat areas allow 5 units per channel.

## Creative scope

- Fixed: Match each person to their own portrait and approved programme record. Retain fourteen speaker and four host roles. On portrait cards the person's name is largest; on the announcement the summit name is largest.
- Designer choices: Choose crops, restrained retouching and a consistent background treatment that preserves identity. Arrange programme information for phone reading; choose announcement composition without inventing registration details.
