# SB3-019-PHO | Cascadia Founders Summit - Executive portrait set

Portrait cards for Cascadia Founders Summit

Our speakers and hosts have sent photographs taken in very different settings. We want them to look like part of one event without making the people look alike. This commission is for portrait cards and a summit announcement; the older square and stage-screen portrait placements in summit_brand_notes.txt are background references, not additional exports.

Please create eighteen 1080 x 1350 px portrait cards: fourteen speaker cards and four host cards, as named in the requested deliverables. Match each person to their own portrait and record in speakers.csv. Set their name as the largest text, then include the role line, affiliation and session from that record. Keep the role readable on a phone and retain the host roles on the four host cards. Use cfs_mark_transparent.svg for the CFS mark.

We also need one 1920 x 1080 px announcement for the summit itself, not an individual speaker confirmation. "Cascadia Founders Summit" should be the headline and largest text. Include the approved event date and a short, readable invitation to register. We have not supplied a registration address or deadline; please leave those out. Do not introduce a price beyond the 600 to 1,200 dollar pass range in the brand identity.

Use the supplied copy and records alongside the current brand identity. Follow programme_copy.txt where it corrects programme wording or permissions in the records. Deliver the nineteen named files.

Supplied identity artwork
cfs_mark_transparent.svg supplies reusable CFS artwork. The older summit_badge_2025_PRINTER.pdf remains a layout reference only; no mark extraction is required.

What stays fixed
Match each person to their own portrait and approved programme record. Retain fourteen speaker and four host roles. On portrait cards the person's name is largest; on the announcement the summit name is largest.

Your design decisions
Choose crops, restrained retouching and a consistent background treatment that preserves identity. Arrange programme information for phone reading; choose announcement composition without inventing registration details.

Delivery and fonts
Use the placement and export instructions listed with each requested file below. The downloadable OFL fonts in Source assets are the current typefaces for this commission; the typography card gives their roles and weights.
