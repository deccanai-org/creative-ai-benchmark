How to read a rule

  2. Each row is one original check on one deliverable. Under each row, Reads as gives the rule filled in for that row: the row's file in place of deliverable, and its values in place of the numbered boxes 1 2.
  3. Compare the deliverable with its Source: the supplied image it is made from (click to open it full size) and what the client's files say about it. Then answer Yes or No for that row, as before. Every row keeps its own check ID.
