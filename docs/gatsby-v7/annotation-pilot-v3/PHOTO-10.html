Northgrove - Apparel colorway image system

Client brief

Northgrove product modules and capsule launch Our range is affordable premium basics. Customers choose between colours and fabrics, so a polished image that changes either of those is not useful to us. The garments should look consistent while keeping their real shape, colour and texture. We need six designed product-page modules, one for each supplied style, at 1440 x 1800 px as PNGs. The 1600 x 2000 px JPEG hero mentioned in northgrove_placements.txt is an older placement, not the file required for this job. Name each style as northgrove_copy.txt does. Keep the photographed garment's geometry and colour. Any colourway name you use must be approved for that style in northgrove_colourways.csv, and its description in northgrove_copy.txt must match the colour shown. Fabric texture should remain visible. Please also make a six-page launch carousel at 1080 x 1350 px per page. Give the capsule a coherent visual direction. Open with Northgrove and our supplied mark, using the attached type system. Close with the exact line labelled "Tall post" in northgrove_copy.txt. The supplied transparent and reversed SVGs provide usable mark artwork alongside the original reference. Follow the attached brand identity and deliver the twelve files in the requested deliverables. Brand identity

Northgrove

Northgrove is an apparel label based in Burlington, Vermont, making elevated everyday basics. Customers shop by garment, colour and fabric, so the identity needs to make those differences easy to understand and trust.

Audience

Women and men aged 22 to 38 choosing on a phone, cross shopping against a 9 dollar fast fashion tee and a 140 dollar heritage one, buying a 32 to 60 dollar piece they expect to keep.

Positioning

Elevated basics at 32 to 60 dollars. We sit above fast fashion and below heritage. The whole proposition is that the cloth is better than the price suggests, so nothing may read as cheaper or as more luxury than the parcel actually is.

#F5F2EC mandatoryThe ground of every product-page module (northgrove_placements.txt) and of every carousel page, as the stock of northgrove_colourcard.pdf, apart from Club Navy behind supporters' crew content; also all type and the reversed mark on Club Navy.

Colour rules

Distribution
Grounds: Off White #F5F2EC is the ground of every product-page module and of every carousel page, as the stock of northgrove_colourcard.pdf; Oat #E4DCCB and Warm Grey Paper #C9C4BA may be secondary panels or bands on it; Club Navy #1B2A44 is a ground only behind supporters' crew content. Type: Charcoal #2E2E2C on Off White, Oat and Warm Grey Paper, as on northgrove_colourcard.pdf; Off White on Club Navy. Olive #6B7248 is the accent for the mark's leaf and small labels only, no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never a ground, panel or body text.
Opacity
Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph: any text panel is a solid block beside or over the photograph.
Not allowed
No colour outside the six palette hexes (#2E2E2C, #C9C4BA, #6B7248, #E4DCCB, #1B2A44 and #F5F2EC) for type, panels, rules, buttons, icons or the mark, pure white #FFFFFF and pure black #000000 included; no gold, foil, metallic or gloss effect, since nothing may read as cheaper or as more luxury than the parcel actually is.
Photography
Photographs keep the colour each garment was photographed in, apart from neutralising the shoot's light, and are never recoloured toward a palette hex or another colourway; no recoloured colourway variant is shown as a product. The tee in northgrove_style_02_heavy_tee.jpg is not pushed to the palette's Olive #6B7248, the crew in northgrove_style_01_loopback_crew.jpg and the henley in northgrove_style_04_henley.jpg are not pushed to the palette's Oat #E4DCCB, and the raglan in northgrove_style_03_raglan.jpg is not pushed to Charcoal #2E2E2C. Palette rules govern the design, not the pixels of a photograph.

Sizes apply at the delivered canvas size, not the browser's zoom level. Tracking is in thousandths of an em: +20 means 0.020 em, not 20 pixels. Leading means the distance between text baselines. It applies only to multiline text. Where a point value is used on a digital canvas, 1 pt = 4/3 px; do not use the image's DPI metadata to change it.
