Client brief

Aldervale Mutual producer counter cards The printer who made our counter cards has closed. We have the last press PDF and need a reusable layout rebuilt for this year's producer run. Please prepare thirteen individual producer-card PDFs and one combined thirteen-page PDF, using the names and record assignments in the requested deliverables. That is fourteen PDF files in total. For each card, use the correct producer and Office information from producer_roster_northern.csv. Reproduce the full Disclosure Text exactly, without shortening it, and keep it inside the card trim. Do not add a district line; the supplied records do not name a district. Follow compliance_note.txt for permissions and restrictions. The old press file shows which fields belong on the card. It is not the typeface, type-size, colour or rule-position standard for this run. Use the attached Aldervale identity and set the licence line and office as body text. Deliver the named individual PDFs and combined PDF at the specified 148 x 210 mm trim size. The supplied press file and roster should guide a consistent layout across the run. Supplied identity artwork No separate logo file is required by this card commission. Use the Aldervale Mutual name as text in the supplied identity. Rebuilding the card layout from the press PDF is explicitly included; creating a new logo is not. What stays fixed Use the approved producer, Office and full Disclosure Text for each record. Do not add a district line. The old press PDF establishes field coverage, while the current identity sets type and colour. Never substitute someone else's headshot for a missing portrait. Your design decisions Rebuild the card grid, portrait crop and text hierarchy for legibility. Keep required facts and disclosure verbatim; flag missing source portraits to the client before release rather than inventing a replacement. Delivery and fonts Use the placement and export instructions listed with each requested file below. The downloadable OFL fonts in Source assets are the current typefaces for this commission; the typography card gives their roles and weights.

Brand identity

Aldervale Mutual

Aldervale Mutual is a policyholder-owned life insurer serving households in central Vermont. Its local producers need clear, dependable materials that explain cover and contact details without a hard sell. Accurate disclosure wording is central to the work.

Audience

Households aged 35 to 60 with modest savings, buying because they do not want to leave a burden. They trust plainness and distrust anything that reads like a sales pitch.

Positioning

A regulated mutual with premiums from 45 to 180 dollars a month. Materials should feel accessible and dependable, neither like private-wealth advertising nor discount direct-response mail.

Colour rules

Distribution
Document Paper is the card ground and the only ground or panel colour, as on countercard_2026_print.pdf. All type is Ink Navy or Slate: the producer name and the Disclosure Text in Ink Navy and the license line and office in Slate, as on countercard_2026_print.pdf; any premium in either. Heritage Green is only the two 1 pt rules that bracket the card. Mist Grey is only for thin dividers or a keyline. Heritage Green and Mist Grey together cover no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area. Palette hex values define sRGB source colours. In the print PDF, convert them with PSOcoated_v3.icc; evaluate the colour-managed proof, not literal RGB hex values inside the CMYK file.
Opacity
Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph.
Not allowed
No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, panels, rules, buttons or icons. No type in Heritage Green, Mist Grey or Document Paper, no premium picked out in any colour, bold or italic, and no burst or starburst callout in any colour, as compliance_note.txt rules out.
Photography
Photographs keep their own natural colour and skin tones and are never tinted, duotoned or recoloured toward the palette. Neutralising a phone's warm or cool cast is allowed; pushing a suit, a face or a wall toward Ink Navy, Slate, Heritage Green or Mist Grey is not.
