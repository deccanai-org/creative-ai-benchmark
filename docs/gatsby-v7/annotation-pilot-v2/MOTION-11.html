STATIC FOX - Thumbnail and animated channel package

Client brief

Channel pack time. Six thumbnails, the title animation, and a feed teaser cut from the captures. The six captures are five seconds each and they are silent, which is fine, because nobody hears the feed anyway. Everything the viewer gets is the text you set on screen. Nobody talks in any of this, so do not put a voice or a caption of a voice on it. A cleared instrumental bed is in the pack as staticfox_bed.wav, and it is the music bed on both teasers. What we need back Channel thumbnails: six files, one per upload title, each title exactly as written in the titles file and each built on a moment from the capture that upload title belongs to. Two of the titles are enormous and four are tiny and they still have to look like one channel. Title and mascot fight nothing, including each other, and the title still reads at 320 px wide in a feed. Animated channel title and end card: one file, wide, six to eight seconds, silent, authored from the identity rather than cut from gameplay. Give the reveal some intent and land on a final frame we can actually use as an end card, with the members line on it complete and all three lines of the end card copy. Channel feed teaser: one file, wide, twelve to fifteen seconds, music bed. Cut it from at least four of the six captures, with the tagline and the upload line from the end card copy set on screen. No invented footage, and no looping one capture to fill the length. Channel feed teaser upright cutdown: one file, upright, six to eight seconds, music bed, from the same cut. Use staticfox_scrap_transparent.png as the supplied mascot artwork, without redesigning or stretching it. Titles, tagline, members line and end card copy all come off the titles file exactly as written. Use staticfox_bed.wav for both teasers, with no speech or capture audio. The upright teaser uses at least three of the captures used in the wide teaser. Title 1 belongs to capture 01, title 2 to capture 02, and so on through title 6 and capture 06. For capture 04, keep the webcam window whole, cover it fully, or crop it out; do not leave a partly cut face. Use the exact members line and all three end-card lines from staticfox_titles.txt. Supplied identity artwork staticfox_scrap_transparent.png is the supplied reusable mascot cutout. staticfox_scrap_render.png and staticfox_patch.jpg are historical references only; mascot extraction and patch reconstruction are not additional deliverables. Preserve the cutout's orange fur, purple headset and silhouette. What stays fixed Use the exact titles and end-card copy in staticfox_titles.txt. Match thumbnail titles 1-6 to captures 01-06. Use the supplied Scrap cutout without redesign or stretching. Capture footage keeps its own game colours. Your design decisions Choose compelling capture moments, thumbnail composition, reveal timing, motion paths, transitions and the music edit. Brand identity

STATIC FOX

STATIC FOX is a loud, fast comedy gaming channel built on co op chaos and the creator's on camera reactions. Around four hundred thousand subscribers watch for the disasters, the bad calls and the running jokes. The videos land once people click, so the whole brand fight happens on the thumbnail, in the feed, at a glance.

Audience

Mostly fifteen to twenty five, watching in a crowded feed on phones. They want energy and payoff, not taste. Income comes from advertising and channel memberships.

Positioning

Mid tier creator funded by ad revenue and memberships. The work has to be loud, saturated and heavy on the face and the moment, because that is what wins the click at this level. Restrained and tasteful reads as the wrong channel; pure noise stops reading as one channel at all. Loud and coherent is the line.

Colour rules

Distribution
Grounds and panels are solid Jet Black, Static Purple, Fox Orange or Signal White, and so are shapes, apart from small Acid Signal highlight shapes. Title words (Anton / Archivo Black) are filled Signal White, Acid Signal or Fox Orange, the palette colours that reach 4.5:1 against the hard Jet Black outline every title word carries. Inter lines are Signal White, Acid Signal or Fox Orange on Jet Black; Signal White or Acid Signal on Static Purple; Jet Black or Static Purple on Signal White; Jet Black on Fox Orange, the pairs that reach 4.5:1. Fox Orange appears in the type, panels, shapes or background of every design, not only inside Scrap. Acid Signal is an accent: title fills, Inter lines and small highlight shapes only, never a ground or panel; leaving title-word fills aside, it covers no more than 10% of the designed, non-footage area.
Opacity
Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity: no tint, partial transparency, blur, gradient or blend mode on type, outlines, panels or shapes, and no scrim or colour wash of any colour, black and white included, over a capture frame. A hard, unblurred offset shadow or second stroke in a solid palette colour is allowed. In the films, elements may animate position, scale or opacity, with motion blur, while they move; every held frame, and the final end card, is solid and unblurred. A cut or plain fade between shots is a transition, not a tint. Supplied raster identity artwork retains its edge anti-aliasing. Place it at 100% layer opacity; colour rules apply to any intentional recolouring.
Not allowed
No colour outside the five palette hexes for type, outlines, panels, shapes, icons or backgrounds.
Photography
Capture frames keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured or washed toward the palette; no capture is pushed toward Static Purple #7A2FF2 or any other palette hex. Scrap keeps the orange fur and purple headset drawn in staticfox_scrap_render.png.
