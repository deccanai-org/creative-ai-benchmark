Astrid Vellacourt - Editorial portrait session

Client brief

Astrid Vellacourt: speaking and investor materials I am preparing Astrid's speaking and investor materials. The photographs and supporting pieces need to feel like the same person and practice: considered, credible and recognisably connected to industrial design. The booking one-pager is a single A4 PDF. Keep the biography factual and include the talk titles and their one-line subjects from vellacourt_bio_copy.txt, word for word. The booking contact is the supplied sign-off, "Astrid Vellacourt - Vellacourt & Sund", set clearly. For the three confirmed talks named in the requested deliverables, make one announcement and one banner per talk. Announcements are 1080 x 1350 px, not square. Use the supplied announcement wording with the correct event name, date and talk title from vellacourt_talks.csv. Each 1920 x 1080 px banner needs that talk's title, event name, date and assigned portrait. Keep titles and dates as written in the records. We also need eight individual talk-card PDFs and one combined eight-page PDF. Each card should give its record's talk title, event, date and format exactly as supplied. Use the template dimensions and file names listed below. The older portrait placements in vellacourt_placements.txt inform the photographic treatment; they are not extra deliverables for this commission. Follow the attached identity and supply the sixteen files in the requested deliverables. Supplied identity artwork vellacourt_av_transparent.png is the refreshed AV production monogram for this commission. vellacourt_deboss_card.jpg is a historical reference, not a tracing requirement. Keep the supplied artwork; the name underneath is newly set in Instrument Serif Regular. What stays fixed Keep Astrid's identity, approved biography, exact talk titles, dates and event assignments. The updated AV monogram is supplied artwork; it is not a font reconstruction task. Announcements are 4:5, not square. Your design decisions Choose portrait finishing, crops and page hierarchy suitable for programme committees and investors. Choose whitespace and image placement within the supplied identity; do not rewrite approved talk descriptions. Brand identity

Astrid Vellacourt

Astrid Vellacourt co-founded and runs Vellacourt & Sund, a six-person industrial design studio. Her personal brand exists for the season when her face is in front of investors and programme committees who do not yet know her. It has to read as a person who runs a company, composed and credible, never as an influencer or an account, because she is asking for money and for stage time.

Audience

Institutional investors, conference programme committees and prospective senior hires; Astrid is between 30 and 50.

Positioning

Founder personal-brand package 900 to 1,800 dollars, pitched at institutional level. Neither an over-filtered influencer treatment nor a stiff corporate flash-on-white; understated is the point.

Colour rules

Distribution
Grounds and panels are Warm Neutral #E8E1D6, Bone #F4EFE7, Deep Ground #16171A or Vellacourt Ink #262524 and nothing else; Studio Slate and Ochre are never a ground or panel. Every talk card keeps the Warm Neutral ground of vellacourt_speakercard_template.pdf. On Warm Neutral or Bone, her name, talk titles and running text are Vellacourt Ink, and the event and date line is Vellacourt Ink or Studio Slate, as on vellacourt_speakercard_template.pdf (talk title in Vellacourt Ink, event and date in Studio Slate). On Deep Ground or Vellacourt Ink, all type is Warm Neutral or Bone. Ochre is for the AV monogram alone.
Opacity
Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity. No tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow, bevel or blend mode is applied to any palette colour, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, is laid over a photograph. The one exception is the AV monogram pressed blind into Bone on a printed piece, which carries no ink at all; on screen the monogram is solid Ochre, never a simulated emboss or shadow. Supplied raster identity artwork retains its edge anti-aliasing. Place it at 100% layer opacity; colour rules apply to any intentional recolouring.
Not allowed
No gold foil, metallic, gloss, bevel, glow or drop-shadow effect on the monogram or on any type, rule, panel or icon: her own mark is used quietly, blind on Bone or in Ochre and nothing else. No pure white ground (never the stiff corporate flash on white). No colour outside the six palette hexes (#262524, #E8E1D6, #4C5560, #B98A4B, #16171A and #F4EFE7) for type, grounds, panels, rules, buttons or icons.
Photography
Photographs keep their own corrected colour and are never recoloured toward the palette: a cast or exposure may be corrected so the set reads as one session, but no heavy filter, colour grade or skin smoothing (never an over filtered influencer treatment).
