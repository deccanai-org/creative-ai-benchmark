Umbra Loft - After Dark event campaign

Client brief

After Dark: a coherent poster and social campaign from six different nights Umbra Loft's autumn programme needs to feel like a series, even though our six usable archive photographs were shot under six different lighting rigs. We want a strong two-colour and grain treatment that actually lives in the images, not a collection of unrelated pictures behind matching text. Deliverables 1. After Dark poster direction: 3 files. The billing order follows the supplied list, and each poster direction bills every night of the autumn series with its date; every act name is typeset in the series type, and a supplied production wordmark, for Sable Quartet or Meridian Brass, appears with it rather than in place of it. The event information is readable. 2. Promoter pitch deck: 1 file. The three directions are meaningfully distinct. The sequence explains the series identity, and the deck charts last season's average draw by month from umbra_room_specs.txt. 3. Night release post: 9 files. The date matches this event, and the post carries that night's act, support act, door time, set time and advance price, with one of the six archive photographs in the two-colour and grain treatment; the act name is typeset in the series type, and a supplied production wordmark, for Sable Quartet or Meridian Brass, appears with it rather than in place of it. The advance price is readable. Treat the nine dates and prices in umbra_nights_autumn.csv as current. Decode 8:00&nbsp;PM as 8:00 PM. The old seasoncard_img_01.jpg through seasoncard_img_08.jpg identifiers are not supplied source photographs: choose from umbra_archive_night_01.jpg through umbra_archive_night_06.jpg, without implying that a pictured performer is a named act. The three poster files are A2 approval proofs, not press masters. Use the billing file's accessibility and licence lines on these poster proofs. Supplied identity artwork umbra_sable_wordmark_transparent.png and umbra_meridian_wordmark_transparent.png are refreshed production artwork. Use these supplied shapes; recolour their ink to Bone White for Pitch grounds. Merchandise photos are historical references only. The file named umbra_wordmark_nightferry.jpg actually shows Sable Quartet: it must not represent Night Ferry. Night Ferry is billed in the series type only, with no invented wordmark. What stays fixed Keep the approved billing order, all nine dates, support acts, times and CSV prices. Use the current palette and supplied fonts. Sable Quartet and Meridian Brass have supplied production wordmarks; Night Ferry and the remaining acts are type-only. Your design decisions Develop three genuinely different type-led, image-led and graphic-led poster directions. Choose the two-colour photographic pair and grain treatment within the palette, then choose one direction to adapt consistently to the nine release posts. Explain that choice in the deck. Choose the three poster headliner sizes above 48 pt; the remaining poster act names stay at 48 pt. Delivery and fonts Use the placement and export instructions listed with each requested file below. The downloadable OFL fonts in Source assets are the current typefaces for this commission; the typography card gives their roles and weights.

Brand identity

Umbra Loft

Umbra Loft is a two hundred and fifty capacity basement room under an old mill in Providence, programming jazz and after dark nights for a crowd that finds us through friends and phones at eleven at night. This autumn we are running nine nights as one series rather than nine separate shows. It has to look like one thing, and it has to look like the room it actually is: dark, close and made by hand.

Audience

A scene literate, discovery driven crowd aged twenty one to thirty five, reached almost entirely on a phone late at night, who read polish as inauthentic and reward work that plainly comes from the room.

Positioning

Tickets 18 to 32 dollars, capacity 250. Independent basement scale: deliberately made and high contrast, credible at a headline ticket without ever reading as corporate or sponsored programming.

Colour rules

Distribution
Grounds and panels are Pitch or Ash and nothing else; Pitch is the main ground and always the ground under an act block. Every act name is Bone White on Pitch with its date line directly above it in Sodium, as the type rules require. Every other line of type, titles, headings, support, door, set, body, billing, table and fine-print lines included, is Bone White; the advance price may be Sodium, as on umbra_seasoncard_autumn.pdf. Pitch and Ash are never type. Cold Signal and Blood are accents for hairline rules, dividers and chart marks only, together no more than 5% of the designed, non-photographic area, never type, a ground or a panel. Any palette colour may be one of the two colours of a photograph's treatment. Supplied production act wordmarks are recoloured to Bone White on Pitch; their letter shapes remain unchanged.
Opacity
Every palette colour is used solid, at 100% opacity, for type, grounds, panels, rules, chart marks and recovered act wordmarks: no tint, transparency, gradient, shadow, glow or blend mode, and no scrim, gradient or overlay of any colour, black and white included, laid over a photograph. The translucent type and rule of last season's umbra_seasoncard_autumn.pdf (printed at 86%, 78%, 70% and 55% opacity) are not carried over. The one place two palette colours mix is inside a photograph, in its two-colour treatment. Supplied raster identity artwork retains its edge anti-aliasing. Place it at 100% layer opacity; colour rules apply to any intentional recolouring.
Not allowed
No colour outside the six palette hexes for type, grounds, panels, rules, chart marks, icons or recovered act wordmarks; a recovered act wordmark takes one solid palette colour. No sponsor logo or corporate partner mark, since the type rules forbid anything that reads as corporate or sponsored programming.
Photography
Every photograph carries the series two-colour and grain treatment: rendered in two palette colours with visible grain, never in full colour, and the nine night release posts share one pair so six different rigs read as one series. Photographs are used as shot, with nothing generated, extended, added or painted in.
